Dogs: Loyal Companions

Dogs are domesticated mammals belonging to the species Canis lupus familiaris, and they have lived alongside humans for at least 15,000 years. They were originally domesticated from wolves through a gradual process where less aggressive individuals adapted to human environments. Today, dogs display remarkable diversity in size, behavior, and function—from small companion breeds to highly specialized working dogs.

One of the most significant traits of dogs is their social intelligence. They can interpret human gestures, facial expressions, and emotional tone with a high degree of accuracy. This ability makes them uniquely suited for roles in therapy, assistance, and companionship, helping people in both practical and emotional ways.

Dogs require consistent care, including proper nutrition, exercise, and veterinary attention. Their lifespan varies by breed, typically ranging from 10 to 15 years. Responsible ownership involves meeting their physical and mental needs, ensuring they remain healthy and well-adjusted throughout their lives.
